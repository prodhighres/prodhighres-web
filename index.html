About

Highres is a beatproducer based in switzerland. The name highres – high resolution means to strive for a sharp, clean and multidimensional character. His sound is meticulously designed by hand often mixing layers or using synthesizers to create rich and unique soundscapes. Each instrumental is a story to be told, while it leaves behind a feeling on it’s own. “Music is a feeling, not sound”

3. Local Storage – Lyrics Editor

This website includes a lyrics editor that uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save your lyrics.

This information is stored locally in your browser, is not transmitted to me through the lyrics editor and is not synchronized with other devices.

Your lyrics remain stored until you delete them or clear your browser's local storage.

I do not have access to the lyrics saved in your browser through this feature.

4. Contact Form

If you contact me through the website's contact form, the information you submit is processed to receive and respond to your message.

This may include your email address and the content of your message.

The contact form uses Resend to deliver messages to my email inbox. Resend processes the submitted information to provide its email delivery service.

According to Resend, customer data, including message content and delivery logs, is stored in the United States.

For Free, Pro and Scale plans, Resend retains email and log data for 30 days while the account is active. Backups may remain for an additional seven days. Transfers of personal data to the United States are covered by contractual safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses.

Once delivered, your message may also remain in my email inbox until it is deleted.

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry and is not intentionally used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Further information:
Resend Privacy Policy
Resend GDPR Information
